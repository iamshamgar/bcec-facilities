Recommended Action

Commission an independent Capital Needs Assessment for 237 Harrison equivalent to the EBI reports done for 120 Shawmut and 218 Newton. Without professional cost estimates, the Deacon Board cannot quantify this building's exposure or make informed reserve or repair decisions. HVAC, boiler, and structural items all appear near-term but have no dollar figures attached.

Eight items require Deacon Board attention, ranging from active financial decisions to compliance assignments. Color bar on left indicates type: Resolved   Action needed   In planning   New item

1

Capital Reserve Fund

Ongoing

Annual reserve contributions needed: $202,868/year (Shawmut $77,915 + Newton $124,953). Current budget: $12,880/year — 6% of the target. The Farina HVAC contract alone exceeded the EBI's entire 10-year HVAC estimate, showing that real costs outpace reserve assumptions.

Recommendation: authorize a structured capital reserve as a separate budget line. Even $50k–$75k/year would materially reduce deferred maintenance risk over time.
2

Newton Sanctuary Roof

Resolved

The EBI's largest immediate repair ($106,400) was completed by August 2025. Dennis confirmed "ALL SET" Aug 12, 2025. This item is closed.

Remaining action: Obtain final invoice and vendor name for records. Confirm city stop-work order (National Registry of Historic Places) was formally lifted before work resumed.
3

Commission 237 Harrison Assessment

Action needed

237 Harrison has no professional Capital Needs Assessment on file. HVAC, boiler, and structural/envelope items are flagged with no cost estimates. Capital exposure is unknown.

Authorize an independent CNA equivalent to the EBI reports done for the other two campuses. Without it, the board is making reserve and repair decisions without a complete picture.
4

Elevator Planning — $582k–$633k combined

In planning

218 Newton: One bid received (~$160k); Embree Elevator declined; Keystone Elevator pending as of Oct 2025. A vendor decision is needed to keep this moving.
120 Shawmut: $422,445 per EBI (54% of Shawmut's 10-yr budget). No bids in planning.

Elevator work requires long lead times and regulatory coordination. Starting the Shawmut conversation now — even for work 5–7 years out — allows time to save, plan, and select a path (modernization vs. full replacement).
5

Window Replacement at 120 SH — $152k

Authorize or defer?

EBI estimated $41,502 for windows/doors over 10 years. Actual contractor quote (Hi Tech Window, Aug 2025) for 77 compromised 2nd/3rd floor windows: $152,000 — 3.7× the EBI estimate.

Dennis recommended including in the FY26 budget. No contract has been awarded. Board should authorize or formally defer.
6

Generator Replacement — Vendor Selection

Decision needed

Fire engineer report (Ronald Horn + Nardone Electric) on generator options was delivered to Andy Te, Thomas Lee, and Enoch in February 2026. No vendor or fuel type (gas vs. diesel) has been selected.

Note: BFD coordination is required for a gas option. The $228k FY25.26 budget figure predates the fire engineer's report and may not reflect the current recommended scope. Board should authorize a vendor selection process based on the engineer's recommendations.
7

BERDO Compliance — Assign Owner Before Dennis Leaves

Time-sensitive

Building Energy Reporting & Disclosure Ordinance requires annual online filing. Newton deadline: September 2026. Colby May (third-party verifier) can verify BCEC's energy data but does not do the filing — BCEC must assign a named internal owner.

Missing the filing carries compliance risk. This is not a capital expenditure, but it cannot fall through during the facilities leadership transition.
8

Newton Sanctuary Lift (Garaventa) — Plan Ahead

New item

The sanctuary lift — which provides stage access for mobility-impaired persons — was flagged as near end-of-life in May 2025, repaired in summer 2025, and passed state inspection in August 2025. It has a documented history of repeated repairs.

No replacement cost has been quoted. Planning proactively — rather than waiting for failure — protects accessibility and avoids disrupting sanctuary events on short notice. Recommend requesting a replacement quote from Garaventa.
